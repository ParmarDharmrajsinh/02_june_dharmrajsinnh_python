Introduction

MediTrack will be a Web app and Android app to help people with their medicine and doctors' routine. It stores medications prescribed and helps maintain stock until needed.

Purpose of the system

To assist people by reminding them about medicines, tracking current stock, finding nearby chemists, and scheduling appointments at nearby clinics.

Conceptual framework & features

  • Reminders to take medicines.
  • Alert when stock falls below 3 days.
  • List of nearby chemist shops for replenishment.
  • Rating scheme for chemist shops.
