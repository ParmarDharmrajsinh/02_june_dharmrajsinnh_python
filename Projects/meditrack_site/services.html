Services provided by the App

  • Maintain stock: Keep track of all prescribed medicines and their quantities.
  • Reminders: Schedule reminders for medicine intake (one-time or recurring).
  • Nearby chemists: List locations of nearby chemist shops for replenishment.
  • Shop management: Shops can upload/update clinic doctors' weekly schedules.
  • Ratings: Rate chemist shops to help other users choose trusted providers.

Constraints

Some chemist shops may not be registered on mapping services; coverage depends on available data.
