Services Provided by the App

Medicine Stock Tracker

Keep track of your medicine stock to avoid running out unexpectedly.

Scheduled Reminders

Set alerts to remind users to take medicines at the prescribed times.

Nearest Chemist Locations

List nearby chemist shops to restock medicines quickly and easily.

Doctor Schedule Updates

Chemist shop owners can upload and update clinic doctors’ schedules.

Chemist Rating System

Users can view shop ratings to make smarter, safer choices.
