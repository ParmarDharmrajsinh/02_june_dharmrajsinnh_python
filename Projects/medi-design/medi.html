MediTrack

Your smart medicine & doctor routine assistant

Introduction

MediTrack is a web and Android app designed to help people manage their medications and doctor schedules. It stores prescribed medications and helps maintain the stock as required.

Purpose of the System

To assist people in reminding them about medicines, tracking current medicine stock, locating nearby chemist shops, and scheduling clinic appointments.

Services Provided

  • Track stock of required medicines
  • Schedule timely medicine reminders
  • Show nearest chemist shop locations
  • Allow shops to upload doctors’ weekly schedules
  • Include a rating scheme for better user decisions

Constraints

  • Dependency on user engagement
  • Unlisted chemist shops on Google Maps or similar platforms

Conceptual Framework - Features

  • Daily medicine reminders
  • Alerts when stock is below 3 days
  • List of nearby chemist shops to replenish stock
  • Rating system for chemist shops
